About IBC

IBC has executed and delivered over 87 projects primarily in India and 8 other countries. The global conglomerate controls 1.8 Billion USD in assets and is present in Property Development, Coffee Plantations, Education, Manufacturing and Software.


Some of the Group's flagship developments include Diamond District, Knowledge Park and The Estate in Bangalore.

We build landmarks and create lifestyles.

Executive Summary

India Builders Corporation is a fourth generation family owned and professionally managed real estate developer of South India.


The property is strategically located at Sector V, Salt Lake, Kolkata in the heart of Kolkata's Central Business district.


The project is part of a 2.2 acre dedicated office development in India's IT/ITES hub. Companies like Tata, Cognizant, WIPRO, Concentrix and IBM have acquired office spaces nearby.

The Development

Millenium City IT Park is one of the most prominent buildings located in Sector V, Salt Lake, Kolkata.


Its fine architecture is symbolic of visionary design which has 95,000 sqft of contemporary commercial office space.
